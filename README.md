Entry for the make.fun bounty "Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown". The program `main.ts` writes a single job title — "Senior Vice President of Apologizing to Geese" — to `/output/entry.txt`. It is deterministic, uses no network and no randomness, and the title is 45 characters, within the 60-character limit. A blind judge compares titles two at a time and picks the weirder, funnier one; this one pairs a senior corporate title with the gloriously odd duty of apologizing to geese.
